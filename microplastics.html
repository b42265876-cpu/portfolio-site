Making the invisible journey of microplastics tangible through a café-based experience.

Microplastics are difficult to see and connect with everyday life. This project combines an unusual ice-and-drink experience, an illustrated fold-out and AR storytelling to invite curiosity and make their journey easier to understand.

Format

01 / Discover

Invisible particles,
distant consequences

Microplastics connect environmental pollution with everyday consumption, yet their small size and complex pathways make this relationship difficult to communicate. Our research explored what people know about microplastics, how they understand their journey, and what makes the issue personally relevant.

The digital experience extends the illustrated fold-out through AR, supported by clear entry, scanning guidance and contextual information.

Iteration

Refining the digital experience

Feedback from the original AR prototype informed three design responses: clearer entry, contextual explanations and access to research evidence.

01

Make entry visible

The poster needed clearer prompts inviting users to scan and start the experience.

Proposed refinement

Strengthen the scan invitation, introduce the experience and provide explicit camera guidance.

02

Explain the AR metaphor

The prototype lacked explanations linking the visual elements to microplastics.

Proposed refinement

Name the topic and add contextual labels, with a collapsible panel for further explanation.

03

Support deeper understanding

The AR experience lacked data charts to help users understand microplastic levels.

Proposed refinement

Add study charts with units and sources. Distinguish published samples from the user’s own food.
